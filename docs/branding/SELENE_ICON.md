# Selene tvOS icon

[Current tvOS master](selene-tvos-icon-master.png)

The tvOS home-screen and App Store icon use the artwork supplied by the project owner on 2026-10-11: a cyan, blue and magenta streaming emblem on a dark blue/purple background. The supplied composition is retained, with only export resizing to the existing 400×240, 800×480 and 1280×768 asset dimensions.

The complete opaque artwork lives in the back layer. Front and middle layers are empty, avoiding duplicate artwork during focus movement; independent subject parallax is not implemented. Top Shelf, launch and About artwork are unchanged by this icon-only update.
